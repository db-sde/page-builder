About IGNOU Online Learning

IGNOU stands for Indira Gandhi National Open University, which is the largest university in the world. It offers globally recognized, most affordable distance and online degree programs, and being a public institution, it is among the first choices of online learners.

With the rise of online learning, IGNOU has acted as one of the prominent pillars in digitizing the education sector in India. IGNOU online courses are backed by its digital platforms, virtual classrooms, and e-learning resources, which offer world-class learning experiences. Simultaneously, IGNOU is also considered the pioneer in offering online courses around 2001.

However, the IGNOU online courses are highly recommended for:

  • Recent graduates
  • Working professionals
  • Tier 2 and Tier 3 students with limited resources
  • Budget-conscious students
  • Students and professionals balancing personal and professional commitments

IGNOU Online Courses Eligibility Criteria

The most important aspect that every student must be aware of while applying for IGNOU online course admission is the eligibility criteria of their respective program. The eligibility requirements vary from one course to another, as listed below:

UG Programs Eligibility

The IGNOU online courses admission for UG programs requires a 10+2 educational qualification from a recognized board of education.

PG Programs Eligibility

For IGNOU PG course admissions, the applicants are required to have passed a bachelor's degree of a minimum 3 years duration with at least 50% marks (45% in case of candidates belonging to the reserved category).

IGNOU Online Courses Admission Process (Step-by-Step)

To experience a hassle-free IGNOU online course admission process, follow these steps:

Step 1. Visit the official IGNOU online admission portal and register yourself.

Step 2. Once you have generated your registration ID, log in to the portal.

Step 3. Start filling out the IGNOU online application form.

Step 4. Attach the support documents along with the application form.

Ste 5. Pay the applicable IGNOU online admission fee (INR 300 /- non-refundable).

Step 6. Once the university verifies your details, the admission confirmation letter & LMS access is forwarded to the student on their registered email.

Is IGNOU Online Degree Valid in India?

One of the most common queries of students is the IGNOU online degree validity. IGNOU online degrees hold strong academic credibility as it is a public university and has received multiple recognitions, accreditation, and awards from apex institutions.

UGC-DEB Recognition

The University Grants Commission Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB) recognition ensures that an online degree from IGNOU holds the same value and credibility as a regular degree program.

Government Job Eligibility

Graduates from IGNOU online programs are eligible to apply for government jobs, competitive examinations, and public sector opportunities, as they hold the same value and recognition as a traditional degree certificate.

Private Sector Acceptance

The private sector has shifted its focus and is considering online degree course graduate as it reflects their continuous learning abilities with time management skills.

Higher Education Opportunities

Students can pursue postgraduate degrees, professional certifications, or doctoral programs after completing an IGNOU online degree easily.

Who Should Apply for IGNOU Online Courses?

Any candidate who fulfils the eligibility criteria of the IGNOU online course admission can apply for it without any complex requirements or an entrance exam. However, IGNOU online courses are highly recommended for the following candidates:

  • Working professionals upgrading qualifications.

Introduction to IGNOU MBA 2026

Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) offers the most affordable MBA (Master of Business Administration) program in India. Every year, millions of students apply and proceed with the IGNOU MBA admissions, but due to limited seats, only a few get enrolled. Meanwhile, the majority of students lack proper information about the IGNOU MBA admission process, which often results in the rejection of their applications.

This blog provides detailed information about the IGNOU MBA admission process, fees, eligibility, last date to apply, and every other detail that an applying student must be aware of.

Key Highlights of IGNOU MBA 2026

The key highlights of IGNOU MBA admission 2026 are mentioned below:

  • University Name: Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU)
  • Course Name: Master of Business Administration (MBA)
  • Learning Mode: Online and Distance
  • Duration: 2 years (maximum 4 years)
  • Eligibility Criteria: An undergraduate degree with 50% minimum of marks.
  • Admission Cycle: 2 admission cycles (January & July)
  • Fees: INR 64,000 /-
  • Specializations: Human Resource Management, Financial Management, Operations Management, Marketing Management, and Services Management.

IGNOU MBA Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates willing to proceed with the admission procedure for MBA in IGNOU are required to fulfill some of the basic eligibility criteria as explained below.

Educational Qualifications

The admission procedure for MBA in IGNOU starts by verifying the applicant’s educational qualifications, and it requires a valid bachelor’s degree in any stream from a recognized institution.

Minimum Marks Requirement

Applicants are required to have a minimum of 50% aggregate marks at graduation to become eligible for the IGNOU MBA admission process.

IGNOU MBA Admission Process 2026

The entire admission procedure for MBA in IGNOU involves a set of admission steps that every student is required to follow to witness a seamless admission procedure.

Step-by-Step Application Process

The step by step IGNOU MBA admission process is listed below:

Step 1. You should visit the IGNOU's official admission portal.

Step 2. Register as a new applicant & obtain login details.

Step 3. Fill out the online application by entering personal and educational details.

Step 4. Upload the scanned copy of the documents, including photo, signature, certificates, etc.

Step 5. Pay the application fee online through net banking, UPI, debit/credit card.

Step 6. Submit the application form and download the confirmation slip for future reference.

Entrance Exam or Updated Criteria

There is no entrance exam for IGNOU MBA admission. However, earlier it was mandatory for students to qualify for the OPENMAT examination in order to enroll in the IGNOU MBA course.

But recently, IGNOU has updated these criteria and has started accepting direct applications for IGNOU MBA admissions.

Documents Required

There are a few documents required that every student must submit for admission in the IGNOU MBA course, such as:

  • Passport-size photograph (maximum size 100KB in JPG format).
  • Specimen signature (maximum size 100KB in JPG format).
  • Scanned copy of academic qualification.
  • Scanned copy of the experience certificate.
  • Scanned copy of category certificate.

IGNOU MBA Admission Dates 2026

IGNOU always releases the last date of application, fee submission, and other aspects a few months prior. It allows students to pay or apply for IGNOU MBA admissions as per their convenience.

Application Start and End Date

The application date for IGNOU MBA admission started on May 15, 2025, and the end date is October 15, 2025.

For the 2026 academic session, the start date is expected to begin in May 2026.

Last Date for Fee Submission

The last date for fee submission for IGNOU MBA admission has not been announced yet, but is expected to be in September.

Exam / Counseling Schedule

As mentioned above, there is no entrance exam for IGNOU MBA admission. So, there is no requirement for an exam/counselling schedule.

IGNOU MBA Exam Pattern and Evaluation 2026

The IGNOU MBA course examination follows a semester pattern, and the evaluation method involves the following components:

  • Continuous evaluation in the form of Assignments (weightage: 30%).
  • Term End Examination (TEE) (weightage: 70%).
  • Project work.

Career Opportunities after IGNOU MBA

Once you have completed the IGNOU MBA admission, it is assured that a plethora of career opportunities await you. The career opportunities available to an IGNOU MBA graduate are similar to those who have completed a traditional MBA degree, due to the UGC recognition of the institution.

The career opportunities of a student depend on their chosen specialization, career objectives, prior experience, and other factors. Some of the popular job roles offered to IGNOU MBA graduates are:

  • Business Analyst
  • Marketing Manager
  • HR Specialist
  • Finance Manager
  • Operations Consultant
  • Entrepreneur / Startup Founder

The average salary offered to a candidate upon successful completion of an MBA degree from IGNOU varies between INR 3 LPA and INR 5 LPA.

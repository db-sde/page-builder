About Operations Management

IGNOU Online MBA in Operations Management is a specialized course designed to impart knowledge and expertise among professionals to manage and optimize organizational processes. The key areas covered in the Online MBA in Operations Management by IGNOU include production planning, quality management, supply chain logistics, and process improvement strategies.

The IGNOU Online MBA in Operations Management is delivered through a 100% online learning mode to allow students to fit their studies in with their professional schedules. The Operations Management Online MBA by IGNOU is recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), ensuring that the standards of education are globally accepted and credible.

Specialization Highlights

IGNOU Online MBA has a contemporary curriculum and the latest study material.

IGNOU Online University offers 116 credit points for an Online MBA that will be deposited in the student’s ABC (Academic Bank of Credit) account.

IGNOU Online University provides synchronous mentoring and assessment with the help of its specified Regional Centres.

Asynchronous counseling/mentoring is provided in IGNOU Online MBA in OER/Video format.

IGNOU Online University evaluates students based on assignments (30% weightage ) and end-term exams (70% weightage ).

A robust mechanism for the redressal of student grievances is also provided in IGNOU Online MBA.

Eligibility

Candidates willing to enroll in the Online MBA program at IGNOU Online University must meet the following eligibility requirements-

  • Passed a bachelor's degree with a minimum of 3 years in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • Scored at least 50% marks in graduation(45% for the reserved category).
  • No age bar.

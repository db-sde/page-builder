About the IGNOU Online MCA

The IGNOU Online MCA (Master of Computer Application) program is one of the most affordable options available for aspiring IT professionals. Indira Gandhi National Open University’s online MCA is a 2 year postgraduate degree program delivered through a complete virtual learning environment.

The main objective of the IGNOU Online MCA course is to assist enrolled students in building productive careers in the software industry and academia. The course includes a blend of in-depth theoretical and application-oriented courses with an emphasis on in-demand computer software development.

Students will acquire a keen understanding of the application of software technology to solve mathematical, computing, communications/networking, and commercial problems. IGNOU Online MBA covers every aspect of core computer science subjects to develop industry-ready leaders.

Eligibility

IGNOU Online MCA applicants are eligible for the program if they successfully qualify for the following eligibility criteria and requirements:

  • BCA or an undergraduate degree in computer science engineering, or a relevant equivalent degree.

OR

  • Completed BSc/BA/BCom with mathematics at 10+2 or undergraduate level.
  • A minimum of 50% of aggregate marks at graduation is required (45% for reserved category students).

NOTE: Students with a non-relevant bachelor's degree, such as BSc/BA/BCom, are required to complete additional bridge courses, i.e., MCS-201 (Programming in C and Python) & MCS-208 (Data Structures and Algorithms).

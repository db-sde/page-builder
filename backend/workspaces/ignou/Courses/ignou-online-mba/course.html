About the IGNOU Online MBA

IGNOU Online University (Indira Gandhi National Open University) offers a highly flexible and affordable Online MBA (Master of Business Administration) program. IGNOU Online MBA is a 2-year professional postgraduate program aimed at individuals and working professionals seeking flexibility in their academic journey. IGNOU Online MBA predominantly caters to working professionals who are willing to enhance their managerial skills without interrupting their careers. IGNOU Online University has structured this Online MBA program in consultation with industry experts to ensure relevance and applicability to current business practices.

IGNOU Online MBA offers a comprehensive understanding of management principles and practices, focusing on theoretical knowledge and practical skills. IGNOU Online MBA curriculum covers a wide range of business disciplines that play an essential role in effective business management.

IGNOU Online University employs a robust Learning Management System (LMS) that includes multimedia content, interactive sessions, and virtual classrooms to impart learning for its Online MBA program.

IGNOU Online MBA allows working professionals and individuals to study at their own pace and convenience with flexibility in their learning schedules.

This program has a competitive and affordable fee structure that makes quality education accessible to a diverse range of students and working professionals. IGNOU Online MBA program combines flexibility, quality education, and affordability to offer advanced knowledge and expertise in business management.

Specializations

Financial ManagementThe Online MBA in Financial Management from IGNOU is designed specially to prepare students with industry-relevant and required skills and knowledge in management and finance domains. The Online MBA in Financial Management comprises working capital management, investment decision and financing decisions, managerial control systems, security analysis and portfolio management, and international financial management. Human Resource ManagementThe 2-year Online MBA in Human Resource Management (HRM) program at IGNOU aims to offer in-depth knowledge and skills for the effective management of human resources. The Online MBA in Human Resource Management by IGNOU includes everything necessary for HR management from organizational behavior, human resource development, labor laws, and performance management. Marketing ManagementThe Online MBA in Marketing Management by IGNOU is aimed at upskilling marketing skills among students in addition to business knowledge so that students can acquire world-class academic learning in marketing. The IGNOU Online MBA in Marketing Management is an online 2 year postgraduate degree recognized by the AICTE. Operations ManagementIGNOU Online MBA in Operations Management is a specialized course designed to impart knowledge and expertise among professionals to manage and optimize organizational processes. The key areas covered in the Online MBA in Operations Management by IGNOU include production planning, quality management, supply chain logistics, and process improvement strategies.

Eligibility

Candidates willing to enroll in the Online MBA program at IGNOU Online University must meet the following eligibility requirements-

  • Passed a bachelor's degree with a minimum of 3 years in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • Scored at least 50% marks in graduation(45% for the reserved category).
  • No age bar.

Fees & Financing
 

IGNOU Online accepts fee payments in both ways, semester-wise as well as annually. Students can make the payments through a Credit/Debit card or with the help of Net banking, but as of now does not provide the facility of No-cost EMI. However, students can seek financial assistance from other financial banks that are globally accepted.

Apart from this, IGNOU Online provides scholarships to students from different categories and has different types of reservations which you may check on the official website of IGNOU.

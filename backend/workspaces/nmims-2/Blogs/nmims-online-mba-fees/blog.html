NMIMS Online MBA Fees 2026: Complete Guide

Compare NMIMS online, distance and executive MBA fees for 2026, including specialization costs, EMI plans, extra charges and total two-year fees.

NMIMS offers standard online and distance MBA pathways as well as an executive MBA for working professionals. The total amount depends on the program and payment plan, so this guide compares the published tuition, instalment choices and additional charges in one place.

NMIMS Online and Distance MBA Fees

The standard two-year MBA costs ₹1,96,000 with a one-time payment. The annual plan totals ₹2,10,000, while four semester payments total ₹2,20,000.

NMIMS Executive MBA Fees

The two-year NMIMS Online Executive MBA (MBA WX) costs ₹4,00,000 with a one-time payment. Its annual plan totals ₹4,50,000, and the available monthly plan totals ₹4,27,500 across 15 payments.

Additional Charges

  • Admission processing fee: ₹1,200
  • Initial amount at registration: ₹10,000, adjusted against program fees
  • Examination fee: ₹800 per subject per attempt
  • Project fee: ₹800 per attempt

EMI, Scholarships and Payment Planning

EMI availability, tenure and final interest depend on the selected lender. The standard MBA source material lists monthly payments from approximately ₹8,750. Eligible armed forces, defence and paramilitary personnel and their immediate families may qualify for the stated 20% concession; applicants should confirm current terms before paying.

Which Payment Plan Costs the Least?

The one-time plan has the lowest listed tuition for both the standard MBA and Executive MBA. Annual, semester and EMI plans reduce the amount due upfront but increase the total paid or may add lender-specific costs. Always verify the current fee sheet and refund terms before enrollment.

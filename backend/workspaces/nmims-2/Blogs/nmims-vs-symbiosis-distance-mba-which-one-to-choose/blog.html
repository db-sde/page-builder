NMIMS vs Symbiosis Distance MBA: Which One to Choose?

Finalizing an institution for higher education has always been difficult for students and working professionals. Similarly, students willing to pursue a distance MBA have multiple choices of top universities, yet they are stuck in choosing between the Symbiosis and NMIMS distance MBA programs.

Finalizing an institution for higher education has always been difficult for students and working professionals. Similarly, students willing to pursue a distance MBA have multiple choices of top universities, yet they are stuck in choosing between the Symbiosis and NMIMS distance MBA programs.

To know in detail about the Symbiosis vs NMIMS distance BA programs, read this blog till the end.

Program Overview

Both the MBAs are offered through an online and distance learning mode, allowing students to maintain a work-life balance and acquire a postgraduate degree while working full-time and managing other responsibilities. A brief breakdown of NMIMS vs Symbiosis distance MBA programs is explained below.

NMIMS Distance MBA

The NMIMS distance MBA programs are offered under its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) department. It is a 2-year degree program delivered through a 100% online learning mode, which makes students industry-ready with its robust curriculum.

Key highlights of NMIMS distance MBA programs:

  • Duration: 2 years (4 years of validity)
  • Fees: INR 55,000 /- per semester
  • Specializations: Marketing Management, Business Management, Financial Management, Human Resource Management, and Operations and Data Sciences Management.

Symbiosis Distance MBA

Symbiosis offers Post Graduate Diploma programs that are equivalent to an MBA degree program and hold similar value and credibility. The Symbiosis distance MBA is offered under the Symbiosis Centre fr Distance Learning (SCDL). These courses are designed to develop leadership skills and assist students in acquiring comprehensive business knowledge.

Key highlights of the Symbiosis distance MBA program are as follows:

  • Duration: 2 years (registration validity 4 years)
  • Fees: INR 74,000 /-
  • Specializations: Business Administration, Retail Management, Information Technology, Export & Import Management, etc.

Career Opportunities

The career opportunities offered to distance MBA graduates remain the same for students, irrespective of the university they attended. NMIMS and Symbiosis are both globally recognized and UGC-entitled, which makes their distance MBA equally credible as a traditional MBA degree course and provides similar career opportunities to their graduates.

The average salary offered to Symbiosis and NMIMS distance MBA programs varies between INR 5 and INR 7 LPA, approximately, and the majorly offered job profiles are as follows:

  • Marketing Manager
  • HR Consultant
  • Data Scientist
  • Business Analyst
  • Store Manager, etc

Conclusion

Pursuing higher education has become a need for career growth and increasing earning potential. Simultaneously, NMIMS and Symbiosis are both decent choices for distance MBA programs. Students are advised to analyze their needs and requirements before choosing between NMIMS and Symbiosis and go after the institution that perfectly aligns with their career objectives.

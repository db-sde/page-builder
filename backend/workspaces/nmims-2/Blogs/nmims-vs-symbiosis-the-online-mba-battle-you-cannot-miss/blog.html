NMIMS vs Symbiosis: The Online MBA Battle You Cannot Miss

For continuous growth and career advancement, an MBA has become essential for both students and working professionals. Simultaneously, choosing from top institutions like NMIMS (Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies) and Symbiosis SODL (Symbiosis School For Online and Digital Learning).

For continuous growth and career advancement, an MBA has become essential for both students and working professionals. Simultaneously, choosing from top institutions like NMIMS (Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies) and Symbiosis SODL (Symbiosis School For Online and Digital Learning).

Both institutions are renowned for their academic excellence, but they have slight differences in their fee, placement, and other aspects. Read this blog till the end for a brief comparison of NMIMS vs Symbiosis online MBA courses.

College Name: Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies

Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies offers its online MBA course under its CDOE (Centre for Distance and Online Education) department. NMIMS CDOE boasts over 40 years of excellence in multidisciplinary education, featuring a distinguished faculty of academicians who nurture aspiring professionals through a KSA (Knowledge, Skill, Attitude) program structure.

College Name: Symbiosis Institute of Digital and Online Learning

The Symbiosis online MBA course is delivered under its dedicated department, SSODL (Symbiosis School of Online and Digital Learning). The institute holds 20 years of excellence and is known for the 51 legacy of Symbiosis. It provides students with learner-centric outcome-based education along with a contemporary curriculum and flexible learning.

Key Takeaways

Students stuck between choosing an online MBA from NMIMS vs Symbiosis are advised to consider the following key takeaways of both programs before making a decision.

NMIMS Online MBA

  • It offers 500+ active hiring partners and 20,000+ recruiters shaping students' careers.
  • 120+ faculty members from IIT and IIMs.
  • 82,000+ alumni across the world.
  • NMIMS online MBA includes a robust curriculum and practical learning to make students industry-ready.
  • Includes a dedicated student success team, allowing self-paced learning with e-learning resources.

Symbiosis Online MBA

  • The course follows and is delivered through a dynamic LMS (Learning Management System).
  • The learning pedagogy includes interdisciplinary and peer learning.
  • Symbiosis online MBA is a 100% online learning program.
  • Students are provided with 24*7 access to their LMS.
  • The course curriculum is developed by the top 10 Symbiosis Institutes in India.
  • For a better understanding, the course provides peer-to-peer doubt sharing and solving sessions.

Accreditations and Rankings

The accreditation and rankings are different for every university. Similarly, the NMIMS vs Symbiosis accreditation and rankings are listed below to assist in opting for the right course.

NMIMS Online MBA

  • Accredited with an “A++” grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).
  • Ranks #95 under the overall category, #52 under the university category, and #24 under the management category of the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF).
  • Entitled by the University Grants Commission (UGC).

Symbiosis Online MBA

  • Accredited with an “A++” grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).
  • Ranks #24 under the university category, #40 under the overall category, and #11 under the management category of the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF).
  • Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).
  • Entitled by the University Grants Commission (UGC).

Duration of the Course

The duration of an online MBA is 2 years, and it remains the same at every institution or university offering it.. However, to make it more convenient and accessible for working professionals and students with other responsibilities, some institutions extend the course validity. To know about the exact duration of online MBA in comparison to NMIMS vs Symbiosis, go through the pointers below.

NMIMS Online MBA

The NMIMS online MBA is a 2-year course that is offered with a validity of 4 years.

Symbiosis Online MBA

The Symbiosis online MBA course is a 24-month (2-year) course, and students are required to complete it within the prescribed time.

Are You Eligible? Let’s Find Out!

The eligibility criteria for an online MBA course are almost similar at every university, but there are some slight differences. The complete details of the NMIMS vs Symbiosis online MBA eligibility criteria are mentioned below.

NMIMS Online MBA Eligibility Criteria

The NMIMS online MBA eligibility criteria are listed below:

  • A valid bachelor’s degree in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • A minimum of 50% of aggregate marks at graduation is mandatory.

Symbiosis Online MBA Eligibility Criteria

The eligibility criteria for the Symbiosis online MBA are as follows:

  • An undergraduate degree from a recognized university.
  • At least 50% of aggregate marks at graduation is mandatory.

The Final Analysis

When comparing NMIMS vs Symbiosis Online MBA, the decision relies on your priorities and requirements:

Go after the NMIMS Online MBA if you prefer:

  • A robust digital-first learning approach.
  • Industry connection through extensive live sessions.
  • A degree equivalent to a full-time MBA.

Choose Symbiosis Online MBA if you want:

  • A globally renowned MBA degree.
  • Flexible self-paced learning with print + digital learning materials.
  • More focus on practical learning with live interaction.

Both institutes are distinguished providers, and the decision should be guided by budget, learning style, and career plans.

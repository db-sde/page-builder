About the NMIMS Online MBA

The NMIMS (Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies) offers a globally recognized online MBA (Master of Business Administration) course under its Centre for Distance and Online Education department, abbreviated as NMIMS Online. It is a 2 year postgraduate degree program with a 100% online learning mode.

The NMIMS online MBA assists students in becoming industry-ready professionals with practical learning experience. The course delivery method includes guidance from renowned academicians and 24*7 access to live/recorded lectures. For better industry relevance, the course is offered in 7 different specializations spanning multiple disciplines with an in-depth emphasis on the core understanding of the sector.

The institute has 40+ years of excellence in multidisciplinary education, which makes its online MBA program an ideal choice to build a successful professional career. For a better learning experience, students are provided with e-books, journals, and lecture transcripts. With the assistance of renowned faculty, the NMIMS online MBA enhances the essential skills among students to provide a pathway to immense success.

Eligibility

The eligibility criteria for the NMIMS online MBA course are as follows:

The interested candidate must have completed a bachelor's degree with a 10+2+3 educational qualification from a recognized university.

The applicant must have scored a minimum of 50% aggregate marks at the graduation level (45% for reserved category students, SC/ST/OBC/PwD).

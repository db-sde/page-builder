About the NMIMS Online EMBA (MBA WX)

The Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies offers a globally recognized professional executive online MBA course under its Centre for Distance and Online Education department. The course is specially crafted for working professionals or executives willing to upskill themselves and foster career development to acquire senior management roles at the helm in diverse business sectors.

The NMIMS online executive MBA course is abbreviated as MBA WX (Working Executives) and offers an in-depth understanding of core business competencies, emphasizing distinctive specializations. The course is delivered in 5 specializations to develop leadership skills and managerial qualities among aspiring global professionals.

The NMIMS online executive MBA is a 2 year postgraduate degree program that enables students to acquire top-tier executive jobs with an immersive learning experience and world-class end-to-end services. The course allows students to opt for a dynamic business environment through the dual specialization facility. The NMIMS online executive MBA provides top-notch learning opportunities with its deep study plan, peer learning, 100% online learning format, and several other personalized student facilities.

Eligibility

Online Executive MBA Eligibility Criteria

Working professionals willing to upgrade their career without a professional gap can pursue the NMIMS online executive MBA course by fulfilling the below-mentioned eligibility criteria:

  • The applicants must have completed a bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognized university/institution.
  • A minimum of 55% marks at graduation is necessary for general category students. However, a 5% relaxation is awarded to students from SC/ST/OBC/PwD.
  • A valid 3+ years of work experience is essential to pursue this degree program.
  • Students who qualify for the above-mentioned eligibility criterion are required to appear for a personal interview, where they must show their zest, aptitude, and leadership potential.

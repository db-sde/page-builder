About Business Analytics

Course Details

The NMIMS online BBA in business analytics is a 3 year undergraduate degree course that is delivered through a 100% online learning platform. The course allows students to learn at their own pace, and it offers maximum flexibility with its 6 year validity.

The NMIMS online BBA in business analytics course develops a foundational understanding of core business management aspects with a blend of in-depth concepts of business analytics such as data management, big data technologies, machine learning, and others.

Students enrolled in the NMIMS online BBA in business analytics are guided by industry experts to develop and apply business models and frameworks to solve real-world business problems. The course is designed in such a way that it enhances the employability skills of enrolled students to ensure a promising future.

Eligibility

To enroll in the NMIMS online BBA program, interested students are required to fulfil the following eligibility criteria:

The applicants must have completed a 10+2 educational qualification from a recognised board in any discipline.

A minimum of 50% aggregate marks at the 10+2 qualification is mandatory (45% for reserved category students).

Examination Process

The NMIMS online courses examination process is similar for both UG and PG programs. The exams are conducted through an online proctored platform that allows students to appear for the examinations from any corner of the globe; they only need a good internet connection, a laptop, and a working webcam.

The NMIMS online examinations follow an evaluation method of IA + TEE, which stands for Internal Assessment + Term End Examinations.

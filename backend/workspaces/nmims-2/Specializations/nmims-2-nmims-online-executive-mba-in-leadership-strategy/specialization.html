About Leadership & Strategy

The NMIMS CDOE offers an online executive MBA in leadership and strategy for working professionals aspiring for top-tier executive job roles across diverse industries. The course is delivered through a 100% virtual learning mode that completely blends with the busy schedules of working professionals.

The NMIMS online EMBA in leadership and strategy course guides professionals in acquiring relevant skills, knowledge, and information to become responsive and successful leaders. The course develops a strong foundation in leadership with the essential course on strategy, which offers a global perspective on today's global business situations.

Students have the opportunity to receive guidance from leading business leaders and thinkers, who assist them in developing a comprehensive understanding of eminent theories and business models. The NMIMS online executive MBA in leadership and strategy entitles candidates to become CEOs, Senior Strategy Leaders, Team Leaders, or other prominent job roles in global business organizations.

Eligibility

Online Executive MBA Eligibility Criteria

Working professionals willing to upgrade their career without a professional gap can pursue the NMIMS online executive MBA course by fulfilling the below-mentioned eligibility criteria:

  • The applicants must have completed a bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognized university/institution.
  • A minimum of 55% marks at graduation is necessary for general category students. However, a 5% relaxation is awarded to students from SC/ST/OBC/PwD.
  • A valid 3+ years of work experience is essential to pursue this degree program.
  • Students who qualify for the above-mentioned eligibility criterion are required to appear for a personal interview, where they must show their zest, aptitude, and leadership potential.

Examination Process

The NMIMS online courses examination process is similar for both UG and PG programs. The exams are conducted through an online proctored platform that allows students to appear for the examinations from any corner of the globe; they only need a good internet connection, a laptop, and a working webcam.

The NMIMS online examinations follow an evaluation method of IA + TEE, which stands for Internal Assessment + Term End Examinations.

Placement Assistance

The NMIMS online executive MBA is a specially designed online postgraduate degree for working professionals who are currently working and looking for better working opportunities without a career gap. Simultaneously, the students enrolled in this program have an ideal target to enhance their skills and abilities to increase their earning potential and demand fewer employment assistance services.

However, the NMIMS CDOE has provided one-on-one mentorship without any charges under its "Career Development Services" on the alumni portal. It allows enrolled students to build a network and connections with industry professionals and get updated information on job openings.

About Marketing Management

A full-fledged online executive MBA in marketing management is offered at NMIMS CDOE for working professionals with a minimum of 3 years of experience. The NMIMS online executive MBA in marketing is termed an MBA (WX), which lasts 2 years with a major emphasis on the core marketing concepts and aspects.

The NMIMS executive online MBA in marketing is an intensive course for aspiring marketing leaders willing to develop relevant knowledge and skills to become world-class marketing professionals. To make enrolled candidates stand out in the global competitive market, it develops the skills required to solve real-world marketing challenges effectively.

To ensure a fruitful career for students enrolled in the NMIMS executive online MBA in marketing, the course guides students to adopt a solution focused approach to real world marketing problems via its 6 month compulsory industry experience or marketing project. Students can also experience on-campus immersion for better networking opportunities.

Eligibility

Online Executive MBA Eligibility Criteria

Working professionals willing to upgrade their career without a professional gap can pursue the NMIMS online executive MBA course by fulfilling the below-mentioned eligibility criteria:

  • The applicants must have completed a bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognized university/institution.
  • A minimum of 55% marks at graduation is necessary for general category students. However, a 5% relaxation is awarded to students from SC/ST/OBC/PwD.
  • A valid 3+ years of work experience is essential to pursue this degree program.
  • Students who qualify for the above-mentioned eligibility criterion are required to appear for a personal interview, where they must show their zest, aptitude, and leadership potential.

Examination Process

The NMIMS online courses examination process is similar for both UG and PG programs. The exams are conducted through an online proctored platform that allows students to appear for the examinations from any corner of the globe; they only need a good internet connection, a laptop, and a working webcam.

The NMIMS online examinations follow an evaluation method of IA + TEE, which stands for Internal Assessment + Term End Examinations.

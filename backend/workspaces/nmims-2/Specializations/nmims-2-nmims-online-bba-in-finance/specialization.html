About Finance

Course Details

The NMIMS online BBA in finance is a 3 year online bachelor's degree program designed for aspiring financial professionals. The course has a validity of 6 years, making it highly flexible and accessible to students and working professionals. It also includes multiple career services along with a world-class learning environment.

NMIMS online BBA in finance is delivered through a state of the art learning platform with a 24*7 access to e-learning resources. The course equips students with requisite knowledge on the concepts and principles, which will help them to cope with the latest developments in the global competitive world.

The NMIMS Online BBA in Finance helps students develop analytical and financial decision-making skills through its curriculum designed by industry stalwarts that balances business knowledge with practical finance applications. Students can study at their own pace and learn anytime through e-books, recorded lectures, journals, and other learning resources.

Eligibility

To enroll in the NMIMS online BBA program, interested students are required to fulfil the following eligibility criteria:

The applicants must have completed a 10+2 educational qualification from a recognised board in any discipline.

A minimum of 50% aggregate marks at the 10+2 qualification is mandatory (45% for reserved category students).

Examination Process

The NMIMS online courses examination process is similar for both UG and PG programs. The exams are conducted through an online proctored platform that allows students to appear for the examinations from any corner of the globe; they only need a good internet connection, a laptop, and a working webcam.

The NMIMS online examinations follow an evaluation method of IA + TEE, which stands for Internal Assessment + Term End Examinations.

Placement Assistance

Online BBA Placement Partners

The NMIMS online BBA does not offer any direct placement assistance to the students. However, it offers access to the alumni portal along with career services, which include targeted assessments, personalized coaching, and professional development tools.

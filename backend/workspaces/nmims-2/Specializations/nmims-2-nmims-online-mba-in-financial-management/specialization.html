About Financial Management

Course Details

To cater to the needs of working professionals and students willing to learn in-demand financial skills, the Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies offers a full-fledged online MBA in financial management course. The program is delivered through a 100% online learning mode and can be pursued simultaneously with full-time employment.

The NMIMS online MBA in financial management assists aspiring financial professionals in understanding the principles and concepts of finance. The course includes in-depth knowledge of the fundamentals of financial management to stand out in the cutting-edge, competitive corporate world.

The main objective of the NMIMS online MBA in financial management is to equip enrolled students with expertise in accounting, financial strategies, risk assessment, and capital investment to be market-ready for finance roles in leading MNCs or business corporations.

Eligibility

The eligibility criteria for the NMIMS online MBA course are as follows:

The interested candidate must have completed a bachelor's degree with a 10+2+3 educational qualification from a recognized university.

The applicant must have scored a minimum of 50% aggregate marks at the graduation level (45% for reserved category students, SC/ST/OBC/PwD).

Examination Process

The NMIMS online courses examination process is similar for both UG and PG programs. The exams are conducted through an online proctored platform that allows students to appear for the examinations from any corner of the globe; they only need a good internet connection, a laptop, and a working webcam.

The NMIMS online examinations follow an evaluation method of IA + TEE, which stands for Internal Assessment + Term End Examinations.

Eligibility

Jamia Millia Islamia Online MBA Eligibility Criteria

Before proceeding further with the admission process, you must qualify for the below mentioned Jamia Millia Islamia Online MBA eligibility criteria first:

The applicant must have completed a valid bachelor's degree in any stream from a recognized university.

Admission Process

Once you fulfill the eligibility criteria, proceed with the enrollment process by following the steps involved in the Jamia Millia Islamia Online MBA admission process:

Step 1. Visit the official website of Jamia Millia Islamia Online and register yourself.

Step 2. Fill in your personal information and verify your email address.

Step 3. Click on the Apply Now button.

Step 4. Start filling out the application form and attach the supporting documents.

Step 5. The university will verify your details and wait for confirmation.

Step 6. After the verification is completed, the university will provide you with the enrollment number and admission confirmation.

Syllabus

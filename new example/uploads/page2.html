About the NMIMS Online MBA

The NMIMS Online MBA is a two-year, four-semester postgraduate program delivered entirely online by NMIMS Centre for Distance and Online Education. Year one builds a common management core — economics, marketing, finance, operations and analytics — while year two lets you go deep into one of seven specializations. The curriculum mirrors NMIMS's on-campus pedagogy with case discussions, simulations and a final capstone, and the degree awarded is equivalent to a regular on-campus MBA.

EMI Options

No-cost EMI starts at approximately ₹8,334/month over 24 months through NMIMS's NBFC partners. Credit-card EMI, education loans and semester-wise payments are also supported, so you can begin the program with as little as ₹50,000 upfront.

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories). Final-year students may apply provisionally. No entrance exam or work experience is required.

Admission Process

  1. Register on the NMIMS CDOE portal and verify your mobile number.
  2. Complete the application form and choose Online MBA with your preferred specialization.
  3. Upload graduation marksheets, photo ID and a passport-size photograph.
  4. Pay the first installment online — your enrollment and LMS access follow within 7 working days.
Note: A one-time, non-refundable admission processing fee of ₹1,200 is payable with the first installment.

About MBA in Marketing Management

The Marketing Management specialization prepares you for leadership roles across brand management, digital marketing, sales and consumer insights. Year one covers the common management core shared by all MBA students; year two is entirely specialization-focused, with courses in consumer behaviour, integrated marketing communications, digital and social media marketing, sales management and marketing analytics, culminating in an industry-mentored marketing capstone.

Eligibility

A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories). Final-year students may apply provisionally. No entrance test or prior marketing experience is required — the specialization is chosen at the start of year two.

Examination Process

All examinations are online and AI-proctored — appear from home with a webcam-enabled laptop. Each course is assessed through internal assignments (30%) and a term-end exam (70%), held in April and October cycles. The semester 4 marketing capstone is evaluated through a project report and a viva conducted online.

Placement & Certificate

Marketing is NMIMS Online's strongest placement track. Career Services runs marketing-specific job drives with recruiters such as HUL, Nykaa, Zomato, Dentsu and ICICI Bank, alongside resume workshops, portfolio reviews and mock interviews. Alumni typically move into brand, digital and growth roles with 40–60% salary jumps.
